Small on purpose, useful on day one.

No framework, no bundler, no account. TinyCart stays flat-file friendly while covering the order work most small stores actually need.

01

Server-verified

Price, name, coupon, and stock are re-checked in checkout.php. The client is UX only.

02

COD aware

Enable online, manual, or Cash on Delivery checkout. COD orders skip provider redirects and enter ops as cod_due.

03

Ops dashboard

Use admin.php for order filters, fulfilment status, admin notes, stock edits, coupon overrides, and CSV export.

04

Webhook health

Queue failed webhook deliveries and retry them from the dashboard, without blocking checkout or adding a background worker.

Embed, sell, operate.

Three moving parts, each with a single job.

01
Mark products

Add data-tc-id, name, price, quantity, options, stock, and an optional HMAC signature to buttons you already have.

02
Shopper chooses payment

The script renders a mobile-first floating cart with editable quantities, coupon entry, checkout fields, and optional payment-method radios.

03
Server and ops decide

The PHP endpoint checks origin, rate-limits, re-prices every item, stores the order, and the admin dashboard handles follow-through.

Cash, cards, and fulfilment in one quiet dashboard.

TinyCart stays small by separating cart UX from store operations. The included PHP endpoints cover the secure handoff points and the admin page covers the daily order queue.

PAY

Payments

Payments are off by default. Configure online payment, Cash on Delivery, or manual collection in PHP, then mirror those options in the widget config.

ORDERS

Orders

checkout.php stores validated orders in SQLite before any payment handoff. Orders track payment method, payment status, and fulfilment status.

SHIP

Fulfilment

Fulfilment is manual: use the ops dashboard, order email, webhook, or CSV export to pick, pack, ship, and update your own system.

POLICY

Shipping, tax, refunds

Shipping rates, tax rules, refunds, returns, and customer support are not automated by TinyCart. Handle them in your backend, payment dashboard, or existing store workflow.

STOCK

Inventory and coupons

Catalog, stock, and coupon truth live server-side. Admin can update stock and pause configured coupons; checkout still re-checks every product, discount, and stock reservation.

OPS

Admin ops

Use admin.php for filters, notes, COD collection, CSV export, stock edits, coupon overrides, and webhook retry health.
