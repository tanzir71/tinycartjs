COMPARE - STATIC STORES

TinyCart Compare

TinyCart is for small static storefronts that need an embeddable cart, server-verified orders, and files you own. It is not trying to replace full commerce platforms.

01 / embedProduct buttons

Keep your HTML and add data-tc-* attributes.

02 / cartLightweight UI

Floating cart, coupons, quantities, and checkout form.

03 / verifyServer truth

PHP re-prices, validates stock, and stores orders.

04 / ownYour data

SQLite, webhooks, payment handoff, and optional analytics.

MATRIX

Pick by job, not by logo.

These tools solve different problems. TinyCart wins when you want a tiny cart layer and your own backend truth.

OptionBest forTradeoffTinyCart angle
Shopify Buy ButtonExisting Shopify stores embedding products elsewhere.Commerce data and checkout live in Shopify.Better when you do not want a Shopify account for a tiny static store.
SnipcartHosted cart and checkout with many commerce features.More platform surface and vendor dependency.Better when you prefer auditable PHP files and SQLite.
GumroadCreator products, downloads, and hosted sales pages.Less control over cart behavior and order pipeline.Better when your own site should remain the storefront.
Stripe Payment LinksFast hosted payment pages for fixed offers.No real cart or server-side product catalog layer by itself.Better when you need multi-item cart UX before payment handoff.
